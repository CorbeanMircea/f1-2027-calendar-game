The rules

How to play

Predict each session before it starts, earn points when the official result is approved, climb the leaderboard.

The weekend

  1. Predict. Before the deadline, open Predictions and fill in the sessions you want: Qualifying and Race, plus Sprint Qualifying and Sprint on sprint weekends. Every prediction is optional, so skip whatever you like.
  2. Lock. Each session locks when it starts, and from that moment everyone's picks for it become visible on Picks reveal.
  3. Score. When an admin approves the official result, points are added. Score check shows how every point was earned, and the leaderboard updates.
  4. Recap. The home page shows a weekend recap: the winner, the podium, the biggest climber and the notable calls.

Deadlines

  • Qualifying and Sprint Qualifying predictions close when that session starts.
  • Race and Sprint predictions stay open until they start. They have full value until Qualifying (for the Race) or Sprint Qualifying (for the Sprint) begins.
  • Late entries. A Race or Sprint prediction that you save or change after that moment counts at 70% of its points, rounded down. A prediction you made earlier and leave alone keeps full value, and saving the same picks again is not a change. It is there for news that comes after Qualifying, such as a grid penalty.
  • Everyone's picks for a session become visible when that session starts.

Times are shown in your local time. A session with no confirmed start time stays closed. If you have not predicted yet, a reminder goes out about 3 hours and 1 hour before the full-value deadline: install the app and switch reminders on from the Account page.

DNF rules

  • For the Race you say whether anyone retires, how many (0 to 5), and name one driver for each predicted DNF.
  • A driver you picked who really retires scores 4 points, or 8 if he started in the real top 8.
  • A driver cannot be in your top 8 and also be one of your DNF picks.
  • A DNF means the driver is classified as retired. Did not start, disqualified and lapped finishers do not count.

Season picks

Once a season, pick the Drivers' champion and the Constructors' champion. The picks open when the season's drivers are loaded and every Round 1 session has a start time, and they lock at the start of the first Round 1 session. Everyone's season picks become visible on Picks reveal, under Season picks, once your window has closed.

  • Joined the league after the lock? You can still pick until the first Round 5 session, at half value (rounded down).

When things change

  • Driver changes. If a driver is replaced for a weekend, the prediction form uses the new lineup. If you had already saved a prediction that is affected, you get a notice (and a push message if you enabled reminders). Your saved pick stays valid, and you can change it until the deadline.
  • Grid penalties. You get a notice, for information. Penalties do not change results or scoring.
  • Corrections. An admin can correct a locked prediction only with a written reason. The change is logged, and the original picks are kept.

XP and levels

Every point you score is XP, across all seasons (including season picks). Level n needs 40 × (n − 1)1.6 XP, up to level 50. Your rank follows your level.
